## Projects

This page will showcase accessible devices designed by the community — things you can actually build yourself. Think continuity testers, servometers, capacitance meters, accessible oscilloscopes, and more. Each project will include documentation, schematics, and code written with blind makers in mind.

We're actively adding projects here. Check back soon, or [sign up for the email list](mailto:babamm+subscribe@groups.io) to hear when new content arrives.
