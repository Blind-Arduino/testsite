## Recommendations

This page collects the community's hard-won knowledge about what tools and components actually work well for blind makers. We break it down into two categories:

## Equipment

Reviews and documentation on accessible off-the-shelf devices — multimeters, oscilloscopes, signal generators, and other measurement and test equipment. We focus on screen reader compatibility, tactile controls, and audio output.

*Reviews coming soon.*

## Accessories

Recommendations and reviews of components, hardware, and supplies useful for incorporating into accessible projects — connectors, breadboard alternatives, labeling solutions, and more.
